1. Account Deletion Overview

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy regarding account and data deletion, users of Splitwiser have the absolute right to permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time. You do not need to have the app currently installed to submit an account deletion request.

2. How to Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account using either of the two methods below:

Method A: Directly Inside the Splitwiser Android App
  1. Open the Splitwiser app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Account tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap Settings → Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account will be immediately deleted and local session data cleared.
Method B: Submit a Deletion Request via Web / Email (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request directly using the button below or by emailing the developer:

3. Data Deletion & Retention Breakdown

Here is what happens to your data upon processing your account deletion request:

Data Type Status Upon Deletion Details
Account Profile (Name, Email, Avatar) Permanently Deleted Immediately purged from Firebase Authentication and user identity collections.
Authentication Credentials Permanently Revoked OAuth tokens and Google Sign-In associations are severed immediately.
Uploaded Receipt Images Permanently Deleted All receipt photos uploaded by your user ID are purged from Cloud Storage.
Shared Group Expense History Anonymized To preserve the financial balance integrity for remaining group members, historical transaction ledger lines retain numeric split values, but your name and identity are replaced with "Former Member".

4. Processing Timeline

- In-App Deletion: Takes effect immediately.
- Email Request: Processed within 7 business days. A written confirmation email is sent back to your registered address once all cloud records are permanently purged.
