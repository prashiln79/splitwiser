1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Splitwiser (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed and published by Prashil Wadkar (“Developer”). Splitwiser is an intuitive Android expense tracking and bill splitting application designed to help individuals, roommates, travel groups, and friends easily record shared costs, compute equitable splits, track balances in Indian Rupees (₹) or other selected currencies, and settle debts with complete transparency.

We believe privacy and data control are fundamental user rights. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used to deliver the App’s core splitting features, where it is stored, and how you can exercise full control or deletion over your personal information.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Splitwiser collects only the minimal information strictly required to operate group ledgers, balance calculations, and secure user synchronization. We collect and process the following categories of data:

Data Category Specific Details Purpose & Operational Necessity
Account & Identity Name, email address, profile picture (provided via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication), and unique User ID (UID) Authenticates your session, displays your identity to group members with whom you share expenses, and links your ledger entries across devices.
Expense & Ledger Data Group names, group descriptions, expense titles, categories, total transaction amounts, currency, payer information, split distributions, and settlement records Computes real-time balances (“You are owed ₹...” / “You owe ₹...”), generates net balance summaries, and facilitates fair group expense settlements.
Receipt & Media Attachments (Optional) Bill photos and receipt images explicitly chosen by you through your Android media picker Allows you to attach proof-of-purchase receipts to an expense entry so co-members can review itemized receipts.
App Performance & Diagnostics Non-personal technical data including crash logs, operating system version, and general error telemetry (via Firebase Crashlytics) Identifies application bugs, resolves native crashes, and maintains application stability across diverse Android devices.
Sensitive Financial Data Excluded: Splitwiser does NOT collect or store credit card numbers, debit card PINs, bank account credentials, UPI PINs, or national identity numbers (such as Aadhaar or PAN). All financial calculations in Splitwiser are purely collaborative ledger records.

3. Device Permissions & Scoped Access

In accordance with Android best practices and Google Play Developer policies, Splitwiser requests only the permissions essential for user-invoked tasks:

  • Photo & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used solely when you voluntarily tap to attach a receipt photo to an expense or set a custom group cover photo. Splitwiser uses Android’s system photo picker to read only the specific image file selected by you. The App never scans your broader photo gallery or unselected files.
  • Internet & Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to synchronize your expense ledgers with Google Cloud Firestore in real time and authenticate through Google Play services.

Zero Ads & Strict No-Sale Policy Splitwiser does NOT integrate advertising networks (no Google AdMob, Unity, or third-party ad SDKs). We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or financial data to data brokers or marketers under any circumstances.

5. Data Security Measures

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Splitwiser Android app and cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • On-Device Sandboxing: Cached tokens and local preference values reside in private, operating-system-sandboxed app storage.
  • Granular Authorization Rules: Firebase Firestore security rules enforce strict user-level access controls, preventing unauthorized users from accessing or altering expenses outside their authorized groups.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play’s User Data policy regarding account and data deletion:

  1. In-App Account Deletion: You can delete your account directly inside the Splitwiser application by navigating to Account → Settings → Delete Account. This immediately detaches your profile, removes your identity from active groups, and invalidates your authentication tokens.
  2. Web / Email Deletion Request: If you do not have the app installed, you can submit an account and data deletion request directly via our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page or by emailing developer Prashil Wadkar at prashiln79@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request - Splitwiser” from your registered account email. All personal profile information, attached receipt media, and associated cloud database entries will be permanently purged within 7 business days, and confirmation will be provided.

7. Children’s Privacy

Splitwiser is designed for general audiences, primarily adults, college students, roommates, and working professionals managing shared finances. It is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete that data.
